INDEPENDENT MINING & METALS ADVISORY

Dr. Sundar Singh Advisor to investors, founders and operators in mining and metals

Beyond the story. Into the reality.

Independent diligence on mineral assets, mining technologies and critical-mineral value chains.

A mineral asset can look compelling on paper. A technology can perform impressively in a pilot. A strategy can sound persuasive in a boardroom. The harder question is what survives contact with the orebody, the flowsheet, operating conditions, economics and the market.

I have run mines, commissioned plants and scaled production. I bring that operator's perspective to these decisions, connecting technical evidence with commercial consequences to help investors, operators, founders and decision-makers distinguish genuine advantage from apparent promise.

INDEPENDENT DILIGENCE

Three questions. Three practices.

OREINTEL / ASSET DILIGENCE

Does this mineral asset deserve capital?

A positive PEA, PFS or DFS is a starting point, not a verdict. I examine the assumptions behind the study, the technical and execution risks that could change the investment case, and the strengths that may be overlooked.

Explore Asset Diligence ↗

TECTONICS / TECHNOLOGY DILIGENCE

Does this technology remove the constraint, or move it?

Technology readiness and pilot performance do not, by themselves, prove commercial advantage. I test whether a technology addresses the bottleneck that limits value, creates a more costly constraint elsewhere, and delivers benefits that endure at scale. Founders get the same straight answer: which claims hold, and what evidence would make the case stronger.
